ViceCityMoments

GTA 6 highlight clips from top streamers, with full credit to the creators.

ViceCityMoments is a small personal tool that edits short highlight clips into vertical videos and uploads them to the ViceCityMoments accounts on YouTube and TikTok. Every video credits the original streamer.

Uses YouTube API Services. Videos are uploaded to the ViceCityMoments YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
