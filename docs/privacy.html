Privacy Policy

ViceCityMoments · Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Summary

ViceCityMoments ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It does not collect personal data from anyone else.

2. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data accessed through YouTube API Services is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The App only uses the youtube.upload scope. It does not read, store or share any other YouTube data (no comments, analytics, subscriber or viewer data).

3. Data the App uses

  • Login tokens for the owner's own accounts (YouTube via Google OAuth, and TikTok), used only to upload videos to those accounts. They are stored as encrypted secrets and never shared.
  • Basic account info (TikTok user.info.basic: display name and avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Public clip information from streaming platforms (clip title, view count, streamer name), used to choose and credit clips.

4. What the App does not do

  • It does not collect data from viewers or other TikTok/YouTube users.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with third parties.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

5. Data retention, revocation and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use. You can revoke the App's access to your Google/YouTube account at any time from the Google security settings page (and TikTok access from TikTok's settings). After revocation the App can no longer use the token.

The App does not keep any YouTube API data. To request deletion of any stored information (tokens or upload logs), open an issue at the contact link below; it will be deleted within 7 days. Stored tokens are also deleted if access is revoked or the App stops being used, and are never kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed.

6. Third-party services

The App connects to TikTok, YouTube (Google), Twitch, Kick and Telegram. It also uses GitHub (where it runs) and cron-job.org (which only sends a timed signal to GitHub to start the App's scheduled jobs; it never receives YouTube data or YouTube credentials). Their own privacy policies apply to data held on their services.

Open an issue at github.com/henriqueabreu314-prog/gta6-clipbot/issues, or message ViceCityMoments on YouTube.
